How it works

Three taps to your own puzzle

No stock pictures, no accounts, nothing uploaded. Everything happens on your phone — your photos never leave it.

1

2

Fill it in

Tap the numbered tiles to drop in each color. Zoom in for the fine details.

3

Watch it develop

Your photo comes back to life tile by tile — then share the before & after.
