SnapPaint — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · WRJ Ventures LLC

SnapPaint turns your own photos into color-by-number puzzles, made by WRJ Ventures LLC. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the app and keep it free. This policy explains what is and isn't collected.

Your photos stay on your device

When you choose a photo, SnapPaint converts it into a paint-by-number puzzle entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, recorded, or sent to us or anyone else — the whole conversion happens on your iPhone or iPad. You pick photos through Apple's standard photo picker, which only hands the app the specific photo you select; SnapPaint does not read the rest of your photo library.

Data collected by third-party services

To keep the app free and reliable, SnapPaint uses a couple of well-known third-party services. They may collect limited data as described below:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — SnapPaint offers optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for an extra puzzle. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising data, approximate (IP-based) location, app-interaction data, and diagnostic data to show and measure these ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat (purchases) — processes your one-time "Unlock Unlimited" purchase and stores anonymous purchase records so your purchase can be restored. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Tracking and your choice

Because ads may use your device's advertising identifier, SnapPaint shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can allow or decline tracking, and you can change this later in your device's Settings. If you decline, the app works exactly the same — you'll simply see less-personalized ads.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, SnapPaint also shows a consent choice for how your data is used for ads, in line with Google's EU user consent policy.

Removing ads

You can make a one-time "Unlock Unlimited" purchase to unlock unlimited puzzles and remove ads, along with the advertising data collection that comes with them. Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.

Children

SnapPaint is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
