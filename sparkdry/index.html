FAQ

When to wash your car: questions

When is the best time to wash my car?

Wash on a dry, mild day when no rain is expected for the next couple of days, and when pollen and dust are low. SparkDry checks your local forecast and turns it into one Wash Score from 0 to 100, so you can see at a glance whether today is a good day.

Should I wash my car if it's going to rain?

Usually not. Rain right after a wash leaves water spots and road grime, so the wash is wasted. If rain is likely today or tomorrow, SparkDry tells you to skip the wash and shows when the rain is expected to clear.

How does the SparkDry Wash Score work?

SparkDry reads your local forecast (rain probability and amount over the coming days) plus air quality like pollen and dust, and combines them into a single score. Higher is better. It also counts your dry streak: how many dry days are ahead.

Is SparkDry free?

Yes. Today's Wash Score, the dry streak, nearby car washes and push alerts are free, with no account needed. An optional Pro upgrade ($1.99/month or $9.99/year) adds the full 7-day forecast, a custom alert threshold and an ad-free experience.

Can SparkDry find a car wash near me?

Yes. The Car Washes tab lists real car washes near you with ratings, distance, opening hours and one-tap directions.
